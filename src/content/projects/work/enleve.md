Developed alongside patients and cancer exercise specialists, Enlève is a pressure-mapped vibration system embedded in a flexible insole that provides immediate sensory feedback to enhance proprioceptive awareness and provide balance support in individuals experiencing chemotherapy-induced peripheral neuropathy (CIPN).
