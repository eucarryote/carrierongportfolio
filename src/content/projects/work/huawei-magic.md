HuaweiMagic is a wand-like interaction system that reimagines cross-device control as a spatial, gesture-based experience. Designed for a future of increasingly distributed screens and devices, the project explored how pointing, speaking, and gesturing could make digital orchestration feel more intuitive, embodied, and magical. The project was awarded 2nd runner-up in the RCA/ICL Huawei HMI Awards.
