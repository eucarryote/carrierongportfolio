Playgrounds is a self-published yearbook documenting the work and culture of the inaugural 2023–2024 Pre-Master cohort at Design Academy Eindhoven. Inspired by the play structures of Dutch architect Aldo van Eyck, the theme became the organizing concept for both the publication and the final exhibition, framing the cohort’s projects as a transient landscape of experimentation and collective growth.

Made with Juliët Nyland and Elena Grippo
